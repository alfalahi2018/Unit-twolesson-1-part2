Teacher plan · 25 minutes

Class: Grade 7 · Unit 2: Awesome animals · Lesson 1, part 2 (Activity Book, page 91)
Lesson aim: to talk about animals and their body parts and coverings.
Target language: beak, tongue, hump, whiskers, antennae, spines, scales, fin, paws, claws, flippers, horns.
Materials: Unit 2 flashcards and poster (Animal body parts), board, notebooks, device or projector, Photocopiable 2 (extra).

TimeStageWhat happensTeacher’s Book link
0–4Warm-up
Mission 1: Spell It!		Show flashcards, students repeat chorally. Students hear and spell the body part by tapping the letters. Choose students with lollipop sticks to spell aloud.“Starting the lesson”: flashcards, repeat chorally, lollipop stick technique to say and spell.
4–9Formative 1
Mission 2: Word Hunt		Find and circle body-part words in the word search (left to right or top to bottom). Check answers.Activity 1: complete the animal body parts, then find and circle.
9–14Formative 2
Mission 3: Complete the Sentences		Drag body-part words into sentences. Students name the animals first. Check as a class.Activity 2: read and complete the sentences with words from Activity 1.
14–19Formative 3
Mission 4: Body Coverings		Read the I’m learning box idea: classify animals by body covering. Drag animals into the table.Activity 3: read the I’m learning box, complete the table, use a dictionary.
19–25Closing
Mission 5: Lab Report		Write sentences about other animals’ body coverings. Pairs read aloud. Exit ticket.Activity 4 and “Finishing the lesson”: summative questions technique.

Differentiation

  • Support 🌱: first letter given in spelling, animal pictures on the table chips, sentence starters in the lab report.
  • Standard 🌿: full activities with pictures.
  • Challenge 🌳: spelling by audio only (no picture), no word bank hints, and write 3 or more sentences.

Notes and keys

  • Word search words: beak, hump, paws, horns, fin, tongue.
  • Sentences in Mission 3 and animals in Mission 4 are adapted for this game; compare them with Activity Book page 91 and change them if needed.
  • Deaf/hard of hearing students: all tasks have pictures and text, and audio is optional. Use a Word Wall with the flashcards.
  • Extra activity: Photocopiable 2.
